# Revona Orchestration Framework

Welcome to the evolving backbone of Revona’s AI-native system logic, workflow architecture, and structured creative practice. This site serves as the public-facing home of the Emberlight Studio orchestration model.

---

## System Architecture Overview

This framework treats AI as an orchestrated engine rather than a collection of isolated tools. It connects:

- Strategic intent  
- Multi-agent workflows  
- Governance and RAG  
- Automated, scalable execution  

At the center is the orchestration engine—linking research, creative development, compliance, and multi-platform delivery.

---

## Core Pillars of the Framework

### **Multi-Agent Systems**
Structured handoffs between research, creative development, refinement, and deployment.  
Each agent has a defined role, ensuring clarity, consistency, and repeatability.

### **Governance & RAG**
Brand voice, compliance, and quality control embedded at the system level.  
Retrieval-augmented generation keeps outputs grounded in trusted sources.

### **Scalable Execution**
Workflow automation and decision logic designed for multi-platform output.  
Supports YouTube, TikTok, blogs, ambient video series, and studio-level creative pipelines.
